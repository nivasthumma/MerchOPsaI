The provider accepting is not the money moving

A refund call returns 200 OK with a refund id. Most systems record that as done. It is not done — it is submitted. A provider can accept a request and apply less than was asked, or nothing at all, and the response looks identical either way.

Worse is when the response never arrives. A system that guesses there either refunds twice or tells a merchant their customer was paid when they were not.

Four steps, and only the last one is evidence

Nothing is recorded as done until the provider has been read back independently. The first three are claims.

01 · PROPOSED

The agent reasons broadly and can propose anything. Proposing is free; nothing has happened.

02 · GATED

Deterministic policy, outside the model. A human signs for anything that moves money.

03 · SUBMITTED

A 200 and a refund id. An idempotency key derived server-side means a retry cannot double-refund.

04 · VERIFIED

A separate read, against provider state, after the fact. Only this step is evidence.

Uncertainty is a state, not an error

Read-back gives one of four answers, and the fourth is the one the rest of the system is built around.

SUCCESS

Verified at the provider. The money moved.

FAILED

Verified as not having taken effect. No money moved.

PARTIAL

Accepted, but the provider reflects less than was asked.

UNKNOWN

Could not be established. Unresolved work on a backoff schedule, escalating to a person after five attempts. Never retried.

UNKNOWN is the reason this exists. A system that cannot say “I do not know” has to guess, and there is no safe guess about money that may or may not have left an account.

What this does not do

A page that lists only strengths is marketing. These are the limits, in the repository's own words.

  • No refund has ever reached Razorpay. Execution runs against a mock adapter. Policy, approval, idempotency and verification are identical — only the outbound call differs — and the console says so on every screen rather than implying otherwise.
  • Reconciliation is a sweep, not a daemon. An UNKNOWN action is re-read on a schedule somebody has to run.
  • 21 of 590 payments are externally mapped, and not one mapping has been confirmed against a real provider. Null means nobody checked — deliberately a different claim from “checked and it was there”.
  • The reasoning model has never run in anger. The default planner is deterministic, which is what makes the evaluation reproducible and also what makes it a weaker test of the agent.
